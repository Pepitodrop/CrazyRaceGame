# Crazy Race 1.1.0 review checklist

- Browser-tab icon loads.
- Rooms receive different deterministic circuit shuffles.
- Terrain colors, symbols, and player markers are readable.
- Piet painting appears as a collapsible easter egg.
- Segment progress is capped at the finish.
- Energy is shown out of 10 and unaffordable actions are blocked.
- Default Compose starts on Snap Docker; production Compose retains stronger hardening.
- Unit tests, Clippy, release build, both Compose profiles, and end-to-end smoke tests pass.
